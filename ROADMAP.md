# Listen Roadmap

> Status: the roadmap structure is accepted; outcome stages and priorities are
> the next owner/Supervisor decision.

This is the only cross-repository roadmap. It describes product outcomes,
dependencies, and exit conditions. Repository tasks and temporary sequencing
belong in issues or Supervisor plans.

## Established foundation

- Canonical product purpose, ubiquitous language, and context boundaries are
  agreed.
- The learner journey is material-centered: use immediately, enrich optionally,
  retain intentionally, and rediscover through the corpus.
- `listen-app`, `listen-core`, and `listen-gen` have independent responsibilities
  and versioned seams.
- Existing learning activities and learner-owned state remain independent of
  Content Package lifecycle.
- Content Package v2 is the current cross-repository closeout slice.
- The Supervisor / replaceable Worker development model is agreed.

## Current roadmap decision

Before adding another feature phase, the owner and Supervisor will determine:

1. the project's current product-development stage;
2. the missing capabilities required for the next credible product milestone;
3. the ordered outcome stages and their measurable exit conditions;
4. which stages are repository-local, contract evolution, or cross-repository;
5. which deferred product questions must remain deliberately open.

No unconfirmed feature list is treated as a roadmap commitment.
