# Phase 2.21 Audible Structure Model (Architecture Lock)

状态：ACTIVE DECISION

日期：2026-06-30

## Purpose

This document locks the canonical definition of **actual audible structure**, its
data model, its provenance model, and its dependency direction. It exists so that
experimental work (duration/RMS tuning, manual QA, threshold calibration — owned
separately) plugs into a stable architecture instead of silently redefining it.

This is an **architecture/direction** document. It intentionally:

- sets no numeric thresholds,
- does not finalize which acoustic features ship,
- does not implement or tune the experiment harness.

It is the reference that both the algorithm rewrite and the local
experiments must conform to. When the two disagree, this document is the contract,
not the current `sound_analysis.rs` v0.

## Why This Lock Is Needed

The Phase 2.20 product framing (rhythm-first, expected vs observed, hotspots) is
correct, but the shipped v0 algorithm contradicts the framing at the definitional
level:

- `stress_anchors` are produced as "every content word", which is text prediction,
  not measured prominence.
- There is no nucleus / focus peak — all anchors are flat `Primary`.
- Rhythm timing and unit counts are derived from CTC phone output, i.e. the least
  reliable signal in the stack (high PER), so a coarse structure is built on a fine
  error-prone one.
- There is no energy/F0; the only audio cue is duration, riding on CTC timing.

The vocabulary was right; the data model and dependency direction were not. Locking
those is what prevents the contradiction from recurring after each experiment.

## 1. Locked Definition

> The actual audible structure of an English sentence is a sequence of **rhythm
> groups (feet / accentual groups)** — each one organized around a **prominence
> beat (anchor)** plus its attached **weak / reduced material** — grouped into
> **intonation phrases** by **boundaries**; each intonation phrase exposes one
> learner-facing **nucleus candidate** (the focus peak when evidence supports it);
> the weak material undergoes **connected-speech reduction** that diverges from
> citation form.

The unifying unit is the **rhythm group**: a strong syllable / prominence beat
and the weak material that perceptually attaches to it. In production data this
attachment may be **pre-head** ("in the MARKET") or **tail** ("MARKET in the")
depending on phrasing and delivery. English listeners use strong syllables as
word-onset / segmentation anchors (Cutler & Norris 1988), so this is not just a
teaching metaphor — it is the listener's parsing strategy. The implementation
should not hard-code a single "strong + following weak only" shape.

Layers (all four are part of "audible structure"; the product must not collapse them
into one):

| Layer | What it is | Primary audible cues |
|---|---|---|
| **L1 Prominence (beats)** | which syllables are beats vs weak = the foot | duration + intensity/loudness + pitch accent |
| **L2 Nucleus (focus peak)** | the learner-facing focus peak selected per intonation phrase when evidence supports it | last/strongest pitch accent; longest+loudest |
| **L3 Phrasing (boundaries)** | how the stream chunks into intonation phrases; where weak material attaches | pause + final lengthening + pitch reset |
| **L4 Connected-speech reduction** | actual phonetic form of the weak material vs citation | segmental evidence: weak forms, elision, assimilation, linking, flapping, contraction |

## 2. Three-Reference Model (locks the "expected" side)

"Expected structure" is ambiguous and must be split into three explicit references.
The whole product thesis (`difference = why it was hard to hear`) is only meaningful
once these are separated:

| Reference | Meaning | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Citation form** | each word as pronounced in isolation (dictionary IPA + lexical stress) | the naive learner's mental model |
| **B. Default connected form** | ranked connected-speech variants predicted from rules, dialect/style/rate, and context (`to` often → /tə/, not always) | the **teachable, generalizable** rules |
| **C. Actual delivery** | what this audio actually did | the ground truth being explained |

Two diffs, two different meanings — keep them distinct:

- **`B − A` (default − citation) = teachable rule.** Predictable reductions every
  native does. High pedagogical value, generalizes across clips.
- **`C − B` (actual − default) = clip-specific surprise.** Focus shift, this
  speaker's reduction, hesitation. One-off, specific to this audio.

**Lock:** the resource must be able to carry all three references (B may start as a
seam if not yet generated), and any "why it sounds different" explanation must
declare whether it is a `teachable_rule` (B−A) or a `clip_specific` (C−B)
divergence. Do not collapse them into a single `expected vs observed` diff.

## 3. Provenance Model (the honesty backbone)

Every structural claim (anchor, nucleus, weak group, boundary, reduction) must carry:

1. **signal source** — which evidence produced/raised it.
2. **evidence class** — how strong that evidence is (reusing the project taxonomy).

Signal source (ordered weak → strong for an *audio* claim):

| Source | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `text_prior` | predicted from transcript only (POS, function-word class, lexical stress, syntax) |
| `timing` | forced-aligned word/syllable duration, gap, local rate |
| `energy` | per-word/syllable loudness / intensity |
| `pitch` | F0 movement / accent / reset (only if/when an extractor is decided) |
| `phone_segmental` | CTC phone realization evidence (reductions, flapping, deletion) |

Evidence class (already in `AGENT.md` and `benchmark-roles.json`):
`gold` / `silver_label` / `heuristic_proxy` / `manual_product_qa` / `coverage`.

**Lock — the core honesty rule:**

> A claim may only be presented to the learner as **actual / audible** when at least
> one **audio** signal source (`timing`, `energy`, `pitch`, `phone_segmental`)
> supports it. A claim backed only by `text_prior` must be visibly marked as
> **predicted**, never as measured.

This is what makes "actual audible structure" honest. It also gives confidence a
real definition: confidence rises as more independent audio sources agree, and the
UI can say *text-predicted* → *timing-supported* → *acoustic-supported*.

## 4. Substrate and Dependency Direction (the core fix)

This is the structural decision that resolves the v0 contradiction.

- **Timing / segmentation source = forced-aligned WordTimeline** (MFA/MMS_FA, the
  validated word-boundary path from Phase 4), plus dictionary syllable counts for
  expected units. **Not** CTC phone timing.
- **Syllable timing seam = explicit.** Phase 2.21 may approximate syllable windows
  from WordTimeline + dictionary syllable structure, but the data model must leave
  room for a future `SyllableTimeline` / syllable-window provider because
  prominence and nucleus are syllable-level phenomena.
- **Prominence source = acoustic (duration + energy)**, with **`text_prior` as a
  prior only**, never as the sole basis for an "audible" anchor.
- **CTC phones are demoted to `phone_segmental` evidence for L4 only.** They keep
  the connected-speech reduction work (their genuine strength) and stop being the
  rhythm backbone.

**Lock — dependency direction:**

> The RhythmFrame (L1–L3) must be derivable from WordTimeline + dictionary +
> duration/energy **even when phone PER is bad or phone evidence is absent**. Phone
> evidence may only *strengthen or annotate* L4; it may not gate L1–L3.

This finally makes the Phase 2.20 promise ("phone PER does not block rhythm-frame
UX") true at the substrate level, not just in the labels.

## 5. Layer Ownership: RhythmFrame vs Diagnosis

"Why a learner fails to hear a sentence" has at least three independent axes. Only
the first belongs in RhythmFrame.

| Axis | Owner | Note |
|---|---|---|
| sound-form mismatch (reduction, re-accenting, linking, compression) | **RhythmFrame** | this is the audible-structure target |
| L1 perceptual filter (a contrast the learner's L1 cannot resolve) | **Diagnosis layer** | orthogonal to expected/actual diff; uses the existing `LANG-009` L1 seam |
| pure processing speed / segmentation overload | **Diagnosis layer** | compression spans hint at it but do not own it |

**Lock:** RhythmFrame describes the sentence's audible structure. It must not absorb
L1-specific or learner-state difficulty; those route through diagnosis/profile.

## 6. Target Data Model (shape, not thresholds)

Target shape of `sound_analysis.rhythm_frame`, reflecting the locks above. This
extends the existing resource; it is backward-compatible (new fields optional).
Numbers below are illustrative field shapes, **not** thresholds.

```jsonc
{
  "rhythm_frame": {
    "generated_from": "wordtimeline_timing+acoustic_prominence",   // not CTC-derived
    "references": {
      "citation": "expected pronunciation / lexical stress",        // A
      "default_connected": "rule-predicted reduced form (seam ok)", // B (may be absent in v1)
      "actual": "this audio"                                        // C (implicit)
    },
    "stress_anchors": [
      {
        "token_index": 3,
        "syllable_index": 5,
        "is_nucleus": false,                  // NEW: L2
        "prominence": 0.0,                    // graded, not boolean
        "prominence_cues": ["duration", "energy"],   // signal sources that raised it
        "evidence_class": "heuristic_proxy",
        "reason": "..."
      }
    ],
    "nuclei": [
      {
        "phrase_index": 0,
        "token_index": 5,
        "syllable_index": 8,
        "confidence": 0.0,
        "cues": ["duration", "energy"]
      }
    ],  // NEW: at most one selected nucleus per intonation phrase
    "weak_groups": [
      {
        "token_range": [0, 2],
        "anchor_token_index": 3,
        "reduction_refs": ["cs_hotspot_id"],   // links into L4 connected-speech
        "evidence_class": "heuristic_proxy"
      }
    ],
    "phrase_boundaries": [
      { "after_token_index": 3, "cues": ["pause", "final_lengthening"], "is_final": false }
    ],
    "compression_spans": [ /* rate-normalized, WordTimeline-based */ ],
    "connected_speech_refs": [ /* links to L4 phone_segmental evidence; no duplicate truth source */ ],
    "quality": {
      "timing_source": "word_timeline",       // locked away from "phone_timeline" default
      "prominence_source": ["duration", "energy"],
      "phone_evidence_coverage": 0.0,          // demoted to context, not a driver
      "rhythm_confidence": 0.0
    }
  }
}
```

Current v0 conformance gaps (this is the gap the rewrite must close):

| Definition element | v0 today | Gap |
|---|---|---|
| L1 anchors from acoustic prominence | all content words, text-predicted | replace substrate; add `prominence_cues`/`evidence_class` |
| L2 nucleus | absent | add phrase-scoped nucleus candidate selection |
| timing source | CTC-phone-derived | switch to WordTimeline + dict syllables |
| L4 segmental | present and correct | keep, relabel as `phone_segmental` |
| three-reference | only citation+actual | add `default_connected` seam + diff labels |
| provenance per claim | partial | every claim carries source + evidence class |

## 7. Experiment Seams (how the separately-owned experiments plug in)

The experimental work (duration/RMS comparison, manual QA, threshold calibration)
feeds this architecture; it does not redefine it.

- Experiments emit **candidate cues** (e.g. duration ratio, RMS delta) tagged
  `heuristic_proxy` / `manual_product_qa_input`. The existing acoustic QA harness is
  an **experiment input, not a product generator** — keep it that way.
- **Promotion rule (locked):** a candidate cue enters product generation only after
  **manual product QA agreement** on real cases, never from a smoke F1 or a
  three-sentence sample. This is the `AGENT.md` Algorithms-and-Metrics rule applied
  to RhythmFrame.
- When a cue is promoted, it must declare its `signal source` and update the claim's
  `evidence_class` accordingly (Section 3).

## 8. Non-Goals and Open Decisions (deferred, not mine to settle here)

- **No thresholds** are set in this document.
- **F0 / pitch is an OPEN DECISION, not a vague "someday".** Decision gate: run the
  energy+duration path through manual QA; if nucleus and anchor quality reach
  product-acceptable agreement without pitch, F0 stays deferred; if nucleus
  selection or accent detection is the measured bottleneck, F0 is added on the
  **production side** (never bundled into the consumer app). The blocker is a
  decision criterion, not a missing library.
- **Full intonation / ToBI model** is out of scope.
- **Experiment harness tuning and manual labeling** are owned outside this document.

## 9. Conformance Checklist

The eventual algorithm rewrite "stands up" this architecture when:

1. RhythmFrame L1–L3 are produced from WordTimeline + dictionary + duration/energy,
   with phone evidence absent, and still valid.
2. Stress anchors carry `prominence_cues` and `evidence_class`; no anchor is shown as
   audible on `text_prior` alone.
3. At most one learner-facing nucleus is selected per intonation phrase; low-evidence
   phrases may expose no measured nucleus rather than pretending certainty.
4. The three references exist (B may be a seam) and divergences are labeled
   `teachable_rule` vs `clip_specific`.
5. L1-filter and pure-speed difficulty are not inside RhythmFrame.
6. Every learner-facing claim carries provenance, and confidence reflects how many
   independent audio sources agree.
