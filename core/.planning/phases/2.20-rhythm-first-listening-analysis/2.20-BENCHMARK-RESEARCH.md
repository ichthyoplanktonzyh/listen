# Phase 2.20 Benchmark Research: Stress/Rhythm Pivot

状态：DRAFT

调研日期：2026-06-30

## Question

Phase 2.20 的方向已经从 phone-first ribbon 转向 rhythm-first listening frame。
因此 benchmark 不应再以 phone PER 为主问题，而应优先回答：

- 系统是否抓住了真实听感中的重音/显著音节或词？
- 系统是否把可听的短语边界、停顿和节奏组切对？
- 系统是否能发现弱读、压缩、连读造成的听力风险？
- 系统给用户展示的 hotspot 是否真的有助于解释“为什么听不懂”？

## Short Answer

没有单一公开 benchmark 能完整覆盖 LLPlayerNext 现在需要的
`stress anchors -> weak groups -> compression spans -> listening hotspots`
产品链路。

推荐把 Phase 2.20 benchmark 重组为分层体系：

1. **Evidence layer**：保留 TIMIT/Buckeye/TED-LIUM，用于 transcript、word timing、
   phone evidence 和底层质量诊断。
2. **Public rhythm/prosody weak-label benchmark**：优先研究 Helsinki Prosody /
   LibriTTS prosody annotations，用大规模自动 prosody labels 做 stress/prominence 和
   phrase-boundary regression。
3. **Human prosody gold benchmark**：如果本地有授权或可下载资源，再接入 BU Radio Speech、
   Rhythm and Pitch Corpus、Aix-MARSEC/ProPOSEC，用 pitch accent、prominence、break
   或 tonic stress 标注校准算法。
4. **Product listening QA**：Buckeye selected windows、TED-LIUM/product media 和手工
   rhythm-frame 标注仍是 release gate，因为 weak groups、compression spans 和 learner-facing
   hotspot useful/misleading 目前没有可直接替代的公开金标。
5. **Later robustness**：IViE、NXT Switchboard 适合后续做 dialect/conversational robustness，
   不作为 Phase 2.20 第一关闭条件。

## Recommended Benchmark Stack

### Tier 0: Evidence Quality

Keep Phase 2.19 benchmark machinery, but rename its role in Phase 2.20:

- TIMIT: phone evidence, lexical stress sanity, boundary timing sanity.
- Buckeye: phone/word timing and natural connected-speech evidence.
- TED-LIUM: transcript/timing and long-form resource pipeline checks.

This layer answers: "Can the low-level evidence be trusted enough to support a rhythm explanation?"

It should not be the only product gate.

### Tier 1: Public Weak-Label Rhythm Regression

Target:

- Helsinki Prosody / LibriTTS prosody labels.

Use:

- Compare `RhythmFrame.stress_anchors` with prominence/pitch-accent-like labels.
- Compare `RhythmFrame.phrase_boundaries` with boundary/no-boundary labels.
- Track false anchors on clearly non-prominent function words.
- Track over-segmentation and missed boundary rates.

Why first:

- Public, larger-scale, and closer to rhythm/prosody than TIMIT.
- Good enough for regression, threshold drift, and broad algorithm tuning.

Guardrail:

- Treat results as weak-label regression, not human ground truth.

### Tier 2: Human Prosody Gold

Targets, conditional on local access/licensing:

- BU Radio Speech Corpus / Boston University Radio News.
- Rhythm and Pitch Corpus (RaP).
- Aix-MARSEC / ProPOSEC.

Use:

- Map gold pitch accents/prominence/tonic stress to `stress_anchors`.
- Map break indices / intonation units / prosodic phrase boundaries to
  `phrase_boundaries`.
- Use these corpora to catch systematic mistakes that the automatic Tier 1 labels may hide.

Guardrail:

- Do not commit licensed audio, TextGrid, or derived rows that violate corpus terms.
- Keep adapters local-data aware: path in manifest, metrics in generated reports.

### Tier 3: Product Listening QA

Targets:

- Buckeye selected conversational windows.
- TED-LIUM sampled segments.
- Local product media selected cases.

Use:

- Manual labels for `weak_groups`, `compression_spans`, and `listening_hotspots`.
- Manual score distribution: `correct`, `useful_but_incomplete`, `unclear`,
  `misleading`, `unsupported`.
- Closeout gates already wired in `scripts/evaluate-rhythm-frame.py`.

Why still required:

- Public prosody corpora do not directly tell us whether a learner-facing explanation is useful.
- Weak forms and compression are often context/listening-experience judgments, not just ToBI labels.

### Tier 4: Later Robustness

Targets:

- IViE for dialectal intonation variation.
- NXT Switchboard for spontaneous conversation and prosody/disfluency interaction.
- Wav2ToBI as a possible weak-label baseline or comparator.

Use:

- Do not block Phase 2.20 closeout.
- Revisit after the RhythmFrame fields and QA rubric stabilize.

## Metric Mapping To RhythmFrame

Use these metrics as calibration tools, not as unquestioned truth. Existing
project scores, small smoke batches, and automatic corpus labels may all be
wrong or incomplete for the learner-facing product target. Numeric gates should
only be promoted after comparing against published research baselines or known
annotation agreement ranges where available.

| RhythmFrame Field | Benchmark Reference | Metric Direction |
|---|---|---|
| `stress_anchors` | prominence, pitch accent, tonic stress, lexical stress + timing sanity | precision/recall by word or syllable; anchor timing overlap; false anchors on function words |
| `phrase_boundaries` | break indices, intonation-unit boundaries, pause/timing labels | boundary F1 with word-boundary tolerance; over/under segmentation |
| `weak_groups` | manual Buckeye/TED/product labels; function-word attachment checks | span precision/recall; attachment error count; false grouping of content words |
| `compression_spans` | manual labels plus duration-normalized evidence | span IoU; duration-density ranking; missed compressed region rate |
| `listening_hotspots` | manual product listening rubric | useful rate; misleading rate; unsupported rate; hotspot overlap with audible evidence |
| `quality.phone_evidence_coverage` | TIMIT/Buckeye/TED evidence layer | coverage, PER trend, boundary MAE; used as confidence context rather than product score |

## Immediate Plan

1. Add a benchmark role field or companion manifest convention:
   `evidence_quality`, `weak_prosody_regression`, `human_prosody_gold`,
   `product_listening_qa`, `robustness_probe`.
   **Repository convention landed** in
   `testdata/rhythm-prosody-benchmarks/benchmark-roles.json`, with
   `scripts/test_rhythm_benchmark_roles.py` guarding required roles and evidence classes.
2. Keep the committed synthetic fixture as the CI-safe scorer/gate test.
3. Prototype a Helsinki/LibriTTS prosody adapter first, because it gives the best public
   stress/boundary regression signal without making LDC access a blocker.
   **Initial adapter landed** in `scripts/evaluate-helsinki-prosody.py`; it can parse
   Helsinki labels, score linked LLTimeline artifacts, and run committed fixture gates.
4. Define local-only adapter contracts for BU/RaP/Aix resources, with no corpus data committed.
5. Keep Buckeye/TED/product manual QA as the release-facing gate for weak groups,
   compression spans, and hotspot usefulness.
